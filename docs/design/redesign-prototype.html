Convertly does the work inside your browser, on your own computer. Your photos and documents are never sent over the internet — not to us, not to anyone — so there is no copy anywhere for someone else to open.

Turn your Wi‑Fi off and it still works. That is the whole proof.

Keep it under

Compression is tried first, so the picture keeps its size. Dimensions are only reduced if that isn't enough — and the result tells you which happened.

Longest edge

The aspect ratio is always kept, and a picture is never enlarged — ask for more than it has and it is left alone.

Order

Add two or more PDFs. They are joined top to bottom — use the arrows on each row to reorder.
